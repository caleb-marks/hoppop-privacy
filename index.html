HopPop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

HopPop is a dictation app and keyboard for iPhone. In short: your voice and your words stay on your iPhone. HopPop has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no servers that receive your content.

What we collect

Nothing. HopPop does not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal data. There is no sign-in, no analytics or advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting service.

HopPop goes online for only these things, and none of them carries your speech or text:

  • Speech model download. HopPop downloads its speech files once (about 632 MB) from huggingface.co, a public host for open speech models. The download starts on its own when you first open HopPop and uses Wi-Fi or cellular data, whichever you are on. HopPop uses fixed addresses and checks each file's SHA-256 fingerprint before using it. Like any download, the host sees your internet (IP) address.
  • Apple's on-device speech. Until HopPop's own model is ready, it can use your iPhone's built-in on-device speech recognition. If those files are missing, iOS downloads them from Apple.
  • The App Store. HopPop checks your free trial and unlock with Apple.

If you turn on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with the developer under Apple's privacy policy. HopPop also keeps the performance and crash reports iOS gives it on your iPhone. It never uploads them.

On-device processing

  • Speech to text happens on your iPhone. Your audio is never sent anywhere.
  • Audio is kept only while a recording is being turned into text. It is then deleted. If a recording fails before it becomes text, HopPop keeps it so it can recover your words, and deletes it after 7 days. You can also delete it yourself.
  • Notes you save stay on your iPhone until you delete them.
  • History is off by default. If you turn it on, you choose how long it keeps your text: 7 days, 30 days, or until you delete it.
  • Recovery copies. To make sure your words reach the app you're dictating into, HopPop keeps a short-lived copy of each dictation on your iPhone. The copy is removed about a day after the words arrive.
  • Dictionary words, corrections, and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • Summaries and organizing notes use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, when it's available.
  • Photos and files you attach are read into text on your iPhone. HopPop doesn't keep or send the file.
  • HopPop has no cloud sync. Notes, Dictionary words, and settings may be included in your own iPhone backup. History, audio, and recovery copies are left out of backups.
  • Your words leave HopPop only when you put them somewhere: into the app you're dictating into, or by copying or sharing them yourself.

The keyboard and Full Access

The HopPop keyboard types without Full Access. To dictate from the keyboard, iOS requires you to turn on Allow Full Access. That's because the keyboard and the HopPop app pass your words to each other through a private shared container on your iPhone (an App Group). The keyboard never records audio and never goes online. iOS doesn't let keyboards use the microphone, so the HopPop app does the recording.

The keyboard reads your contact names and text replacements from iOS so it doesn't autocorrect them. It doesn't save or send them. HopPop doesn't monitor or keep what you type in other apps.

Purchases

HopPop includes a free 14-day trial, then a one-time $19.99 unlock (the price may vary by country). There is no subscription. Apple handles all payments, and HopPop never sees your payment details. On your iPhone, HopPop stores only whether you've unlocked it and when your trial started.

Children

HopPop is not directed at children under 13. HopPop collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
