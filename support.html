HopPop Support

HopPop is private, on-device dictation and a keyboard for iPhone. It requires iOS 26 or later.

Contact

Questions, bugs, or feedback: caleblmarks25@gmail.com.

For a bug, include your iPhone model, iOS version, HopPop version, and the steps that caused it. Do not include private dictated text or recordings unless you choose to share them.

Set up the keyboard

  1. Open iPhone Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › HopPop.
  2. For dictation, turn on Allow Full Access for HopPop. Typing works without it. Full Access lets the keyboard and app exchange your words and settings on this iPhone; the keyboard never uses the network.
  3. In a text field, choose the HopPop keyboard from the keyboard switcher at least once. This lets HopPop know its keyboard is in use.
  4. Open HopPop and allow microphone access. It prepares the microphone for the keyboard. Return to the app where you want to type and select the HopPop keyboard.
  5. Tap the keyboard mic, speak, then tap again to stop. While the mic session is ready, later dictations can start from the keyboard. If the keyboard asks you to open HopPop, open it from the Home Screen and return to your app. iOS may decline an attempt to open it from the keyboard.

If dictation does not start

  • Microphone access: check iPhone Settings › Apps › HopPop › Microphone.
  • Speech download: open HopPop and let its one-time download finish. It uses about 632 MB and may use cellular data. Until then, HopPop can use Apple's on-device speech if it is available for your iPhone and language.
  • Keyboard session ended: open HopPop again to prepare the mic, then return to your app. Sessions end after 15 minutes without another recording. The orange microphone indicator shows while the mic is ready.
  • Stop the mic session: use End on HopPop's Live Activity, End Session in Control Center, or End on the keyboard.

Purchases

Your first 100 dictations are free. After that, a one-time unlock keeps dictation available. There is no subscription. Typing and History continue to work without the unlock.

To restore an existing purchase, open HopPop Settings › Purchases › Restore purchase, or tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen. Use the Apple Account that bought the unlock.

Smart cleanup

In-app cleanup needs a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model ready. If cleanup is unavailable, your original words stay. Tap Original to return to your exact words after cleanup.

The keyboard Prompt button is available only to earlier installations that already downloaded HopPop's previous on-device Smart model. New installations do not offer this model download or button.

History and recovery

New installations keep transcript history for 7 days. Turning history off deletes saved history. Undelivered words remain in recovery until you send, copy, or discard them. Interrupted recordings can be recovered for up to 7 days.
