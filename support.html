Quick help

  • Add the keyboard: Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › HopPop.
  • Dictate from the keyboard: turn on Allow Full Access for HopPop in the same place. The keyboard uses it only to pass your words to and from the HopPop app on your iPhone.
  • First mic tap opens HopPop: iOS doesn't let keyboards use the microphone, so HopPop records for the keyboard. Swipe back to your app; the next taps record without leaving.
  • Restore your purchase: tap Restore purchase on HopPopopen HopPop and tap Restore Purchases on the unlock screen.rsquo;s unlock screen or in HopPop Settings.
